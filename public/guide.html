WRC Hostel User Manual

🍱 Canteen Tracker Guide

Quick reference guide for tracking meals & calculating monthly bills

📅 1. Nepali Bikram Sambat (BS) Calendar

The app displays dates in Bikram Sambat (e.g., Bhadra 27, 2083) so it matches the hostel canteen ledger exactly.

  • Use Previous / Next buttons to navigate days.
  • Tap Today anytime to jump back to today's date.

⚡ 2. Auto-Save vs. Manual Confirmation

  • Today's Date: Always automatically saves as you record meals.
  • Past & Future Dates: When you browse to a past or future date, it opens in Draft Mode. It only saves to your bill when you tap the Tick (✓ Save Day) button!
  • Auto-Save Feature: If enabled in Settings, the app automatically fills and saves missed days as per your default meals even when you don't open the app.

🥐 3. Meals, Breakfast & Addons

  • Morning Food & Dinner: Tap to toggle between Eaten and Skipped.
  • Breakfast: By default marked as not eaten. When eaten, tap quick-tap presets (Chowmein, Momo, Tea) or type a custom item & price.
  • Masu & Omelette: Use [ - ] and [ + ] buttons to adjust quantities.
  • Custom Food Options: You can add custom items (like Milk or Snacks) in Settings!

📊 4. Monthly Bill Summary

  • Open the Monthly Bill tab to inspect the total expected bill for the Nepali month.
  • Expand any day in the Daily Records list to verify every single dish against the canteen ledger.
  • Use Close Month to save a permanent snapshot when settling bills.

⚙️ 5. Customization & Backup

  • Removable Options: You can disable or delete ANY food item in Settings (even Morning Food or Dinner) if you don't eat it.
  • Backup: Export your data as a JSON file anytime to protect against browser data clears.
💡 Pro Tip: Install this app on your phone home screen using your browser's "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App" menu for full offline PWA access!

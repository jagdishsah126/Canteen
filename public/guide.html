🍱 Canteen Tracker Guide

Complete step-by-step manual for tracking meals and calculating hostel monthly bills

1

📱 Open & Install the App (Shortcut)

First step, open the app link in your phone browser:

https://canteen-wrc.vercel.app/ ↗
  • On Android (Chrome / Brave / Edge): Tap the 3 vertical dots (⋮) menu at the top-right corner, then tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  • On iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button (⎋) at the bottom, scroll down, and tap "Add to Home Screen".
🎉 App is ready to work now! An icon will appear right on your phone's home screen. The app is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that operates 100% offline without any server or internet connection needed!
2

⚙️ Go to Settings & Customize as You Wish

Open the Settings tab at the bottom navigation bar to tailor the app to your hostel routine:

  • 💰 Adjust Core Rates: Update default meal prices if rates change (Morning Food Rs. 72, Dinner Rs. 72, Masu Rs. 75, Omelette Rs. 30). Existing past bills remain frozen!
  • 🗑️ Remove or Hide Meals: Don't take Dinner, Masu, or Breakfast? You can disable any core meal from your daily feed with one tap. Tap ↺ Reset Defaults anytime to restore them.
  • ➕ Add Custom Options: Want to track Milk (दूध), Afternoon Snacks, or extra Roti? Tap + Add Custom Option:
    • Toggle: Yes/No eaten checkbox (e.g. Milk).
    • Quantity: Number stepper (e.g. Extra Roti).
    • Multi-Choice with Presets: Dropdown items like Breakfast (e.g. Chowmein, Momo, Tea).
  • ⚡ Auto-Save Daily Records: Turn this ON if you eat standard meals every day. Missed days will automatically update with default meals even if you forget to open the app!
3

📅 Backfill Current Month's Data

If you're starting in the middle of a Nepali Bikram Sambat (BS) month (e.g., Bhadra):

  • Use the [ ‹ Prev Day ] arrow on the Home tab to navigate to earlier days of this month.
  • Mark what you ate on each past day (Morning Food, Dinner, Breakfast item, or Addons).
  • Click the green ✓ Save Day (Tick) button at the top header to save each past day into your ledger!
  • Repeat for the elapsed days of the current month so your total bill matches your hostel's master ledger.
💡 Remember: Browsing past or future days operates in Draft Mode. They are only added to your bill when you click the Tick (✓) button!
4

🚀 Ready to Go! Your Daily Habit

Now you are completely set up and ready to go:

  • Today is always auto-saved: Whenever you open the app on today's date, changes save automatically.
  • Eat standard meals? You don't have to do anything! If Auto-Save is ON, standard Morning Food and Dinner are recorded automatically.
  • Did something different? Skipped dinner, had a plate of Momo, or added 2 Masu? Just open the app, tap once to edit it, and you're done!
  • End-of-Month Audit: Tap the Monthly Bill tab to inspect your itemized summary day-by-day and compare against the canteen ledger before paying.
5
